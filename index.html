Caption Note

Reads live captions from Teams, Zoom, or Google Meet via screen share + OCR, then saves them as a note. Does not use Record or Transcribe APIs.

  1. Turn on live captions in your meeting app (Teams, Zoom, or Google Meet).
  2. Pop captions out into their own window if the app allows it.
  3. Use Chrome or Edge. On a Mac, allow Screen Recording for that browser in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  4. Pick your platform below, click Start, then share the captions window. If the preview is black, share the entire screen instead — some meeting windows capture black on macOS.
Not recording.

Auto detects a short captions popup vs a tall meeting/screen and crops accordingly.
