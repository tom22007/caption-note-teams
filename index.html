Caption Note

Reads live captions from your screen and saves them as a note. Does not use Record or Transcribe.

  1. Turn on live captions in Teams: More (…) → Language and speech → Show live captions.
  2. Pop captions out into their own window if you can.
  3. Use Chrome or Edge. On a Mac, allow Screen Recording for that browser in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  4. Click Start, then share the Captions window. If the preview is black, share the entire screen instead — Teams windows often capture black.
